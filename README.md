## Masco Corporation (masco)

Masco Corporation is a global leader in the design, manufacture, and distribution of branded home improvement and building products, including faucets, plumbing fixtures, cabinets, paints, and architectural coatings under brands such as Delta, Behr, Hansgrohe, KILZ, and Liberty Hardware.
